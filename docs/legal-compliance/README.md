# Legal Compliance

## Overview

AI PoliceNet must comply with applicable laws, constitutional protections, and privacy regulations. This document outlines the legal framework and compliance requirements.

## Governing Laws & Regulations

### India
- Information Technology Act, 2000 (and amendments)
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
- Indian Penal Code / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita
- Code of Criminal Procedure / Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita
- Right to Information Act, 2005
- Indian Evidence Act / Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam

### Key Compliance Areas

1. **Data Protection**
   - Collect only necessary personal data
   - Obtain informed consent for data collection
   - Provide data access and deletion rights
   - Implement data retention policies
   - Cross-border data transfer restrictions

2. **Evidence Admissibility**
   - Digital evidence must meet legal standards
   - Chain of custody must be maintained
   - Hash verification for evidence integrity
   - Proper authentication of digital records

3. **Surveillance & Monitoring**
   - Social media monitoring must follow legal authorization
   - No unauthorized mass surveillance
   - Proper legal process for content takedown
   - Respect for freedom of speech and expression

4. **Due Process**
   - AI recommendations require human authorization
   - No automated penalties without legal proceedings
   - Right to appeal for all parties
   - Transparent decision-making processes

5. **Government Employee Accountability**
   - Fines and disciplinary actions follow established procedures
   - Officers have the right to defense and appeal
   - Misconduct findings require proper investigation
   - Records maintained with full audit trail

## Privacy by Design

- Data minimization at collection
- Purpose limitation for all processing
- Storage limitation with automatic purging
- Encryption by default
- Anonymous reporting options for citizens
